Reading Frame

Research papers, explained in about a minute. Each video walks through a paper's own figures and adds one custom animated schematic for the idea the figures are evidence for.

What this is

Reading Frame is a small science-communication project. Papers in microbial ecology, synthetic biology and related fields are turned into short vertical videos aimed at scientists in neighbouring fields, students, and anyone curious enough to want the real evidence rather than a headline. Every number quoted in a video is checked against the paper it came from, and each video cites the paper and its DOI.

How a video is made

  1. The paper's text and figures are extracted from the PDF.
  2. A script is written around one central concept, with each claim tied to a quote from the paper.
  3. Narration is produced with a synthetic voice. No video contains a recording of a real person.
  4. Animations are rendered with Manim; the paper's own figures are shown and credited.
  5. Frames are reviewed and re-rendered before anything is published.

Where to watch

Videos are published on YouTube as Reading Frame.
