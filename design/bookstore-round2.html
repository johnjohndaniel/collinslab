Open questions for round 3

Things I'd like your eye on

S1 · windowShould the window books be tappable straight into the book view (as now), or open a "this week's window" shelf first?
S1 · sectionsSection "doors" as small illustrated shelf cards (as drawn), or simple named tiles?
S2 · pokeOne tap pulls a book out with a tag, a second tap picks it up. Natural, or should one tap go straight to the book?
S3 · book viewHow much shop should show behind the book: a blurred shelf (as drawn), the actual gap where it sat, or plain darkness?
S3 · detailsPrice and Add to bag in a bottom sheet (as drawn), or only after you've turned the book over?
VoiceThe notes are signed "M." (a shopkeeper) in these drawings as a placeholder. Keep a named shopkeeper, or use your own voice?
